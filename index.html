📱 App Revenue Optimization & Monetization

Analyzed mobile app data to improve performance and increase revenue. This included studying onboarding flows, identifying high-performing features, understanding in-app purchase behavior, benchmarking competitors, and optimizing ad monetization with Google AdMob.
